THE STORY

Born in Iran. Living freely in Israel.

Persian heritage · Jewish symbolism · One modern identity

Shervinah was born and raised in Iran until the age of fourteen and now lives freely in Israel. As an inspirational speaker and spiritual guide, she opens a personal window onto both cultures, through language, traditions, food, faith and everyday encounters.

Her work brings together numerology, Kabbalah, spirituality and soul healing. At its heart is a message of courage: women deserve a voice, people deserve freedom, and Iranians and Israelis can meet through humanity, curiosity and hope.
