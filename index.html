THE STORY

Two cultures. One voice.

Persian heritage · Jewish symbolism · One modern identity

Shervinah’s world is shaped by Persian memory and Israeli everyday life. Her work is about seeing beyond headlines — into food, language, streets, rituals, art, music and the small moments that make a place feel human.

This space was created for curiosity: to discover Israel through beauty and culture, while keeping a deep connection to Persian identity and heritage.
